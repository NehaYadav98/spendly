Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

01Data We Collect

Spendly only collects what it needs to help you track your spending:

  • Account details, such as your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense records you enter, including amount, category, date, and description.
  • Basic technical information, such as your browser type, needed to keep the service running.

02How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve Spendly, for example to show your expense history, category breakdowns, and monthly summaries. We do not sell your personal information or use your expense data for advertising.

03Data Storage

Your information is stored in our database and protected with reasonable security measures. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and you can delete your expenses or your account at any time.

04Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third parties, except where required by law. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive standard request information such as your IP address.

05Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at support@spendly.example. See also our Terms and Conditions.
